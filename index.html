Cosy support

Cosy is a calm baby tracker for two parents: feeds, nappies, sleep, medicines, doctor visits and firsts, shared through your own iCloud.

Get help

Email hi@arpitbansal.com, or use Settings → Ideas & feedback in the app. Every message is read.

Common questions

How do I share with my partner?
Settings → Invite partner. They open the link on their iPhone and join with their Apple Account.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
No, if iCloud is on for Cosy. Reinstall with the same Apple Account and it comes back. Settings → Backup shows what's saved.

My partner's entries aren't showing.
Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud with iCloud on for Cosy, and open Cosy once. Low Power Mode can delay updates.

Is Cosy medical advice?
No. Cosy is a diary and helper. It never suggests doses; always follow your doctor.
