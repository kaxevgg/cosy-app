Cosy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Cosy is a baby diary for parents. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • We don't have your baby's log. Cosy has no accounts on our side. Your family's log is never sent to us or kept by us.
  • Your family's log lives in your own iCloud. When you invite your partner, Cosy shares the log directly between your Apple Accounts using Apple's iCloud sharing. Only the people you invite can see it.
  • Sound and photos stay on your iPhone or iPad. Recognising crying, spotting wake-ups, reading food photos and finding baby photos in your library all happen on your device.
  • Answers, summaries, the health overview and stories use Google's Gemini, once you agree. Cosy asks before the first time, removes names and other personal details first, and you can switch to the model on your iPhone or iPad, or say "Never", at any time. See "Gemini" below.
  • One small relay, for instant notifications. So that a feed or sleep timer appears on your partner's phone straight away, Cosy uses a small relay that keeps only push addresses. See "The notification relay" below.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Ever. Cosy only sends anonymous usage counts (see below), and you can switch those off.

Gemini

Cosy uses Google's Gemini, through Google Firebase, as its default model for the things a model does better than simple rules. It is used only after you agree in the app; until then, and if you choose "On this iPhone" ("On this iPad" on iPad) or "Never" in Settings → Assistant, nothing is sent and Cosy uses Apple's on-device model where your device has one.

What is sent, and when:

  • Reading a sentence into log entries: the words you typed or said, and your medicine names. Nothing from your log.
  • Answers in Ask: your question, your child's age, and the parts of the log you allowed Ask to read (for example feeds, sleep and nappies by day), with your child's name replaced. The exact birth date is not sent.
  • Summaries of doctor reports: the text of the report. Before it is sent, Cosy removes family names, other people's names, birth dates, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses and ID or record numbers. This removal is automatic and works on scanned text, so it can occasionally miss something a scan misread; the name of a hospital or clinic printed on a report may remain. Family papers (birth certificate, passports, insurance) are never sent.
  • The health overview: your child's (or your) age; doctor visits with their dates, notes, what the doctor said and the questions you asked; medicines with their doses and how many were given in the last 30 days; vaccines given and due; growth measurements; the summaries of your reports; and the previous overview, so it can say what changed. Doctors' and clinics' names are left out, and the same personal details as above are removed first. Counts such as visits in the last six months are worked out on your iPhone or iPad, not by Gemini.
  • Letters Cosy drafts: the week's or month's totals and the firsts you recorded, with names replaced by stand-ins.
  • Bedtime stories: the theme, length and language you picked and anything you typed as an idea. Your child's name is added on your iPhone or iPad afterwards.

Cosy uses Gemini on a paid plan, under which Google does not use what is sent to train its models or improve its products. Requests are checked with Apple's App Attest so only the genuine Cosy app can make them; no account, name or device identifier from Cosy is attached. Google's handling is covered by the Google Cloud and Firebase terms. Answers and summaries can contain mistakes and are not medical advice.

The notification relay

iCloud cannot make a timer appear on another phone's Lock Screen while the app is closed; Apple requires a server to send that push. For this Cosy uses one small relay (a Cloudflare Worker). It is used for three things: showing a feed or sleep timer on your family's other phones the moment it starts, passing on "crying heard" alerts from the baby monitor, and silently waking your phone before story time if you turned on the nightly story.

  • What it keeps: for each family, a random family id, each phone's push addresses (issued by Apple; they don't say who you are), the date the phone last registered and, if the nightly story is on, the times of day to wake that phone. Addresses are forgotten 60 days after a phone last used Cosy.
  • What passes through and is not kept: the timer's content (your child's first name, which side, the start time) and the text of a crying alert, on their way to Apple's push service.
  • What it never gets: your log, history, photos, clips, reports, health overviews, questions or stories.

Lullabies and white noise (YouTube)

The Bedtime screen plays a fixed list of lullaby and white-noise videos using YouTube's own player inside Cosy. When you play one, your iPhone or iPad connects to YouTube, which receives what any video player would (such as your IP address and which video was played) under Google's privacy policy, and may show its own adverts. Cosy sends YouTube nothing about your family and keeps no YouTube cookies between sessions.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of Cosy are useful, the app sends anonymous counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. A count looks like "a feed was logged", "Memories was opened" or "Log or ask was used by voice". Each one also carries the app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, language and region, time zone, and accessibility settings such as text size.

They never include names, notes, foods, amounts, times, questions, medicine names, photos or anything else from your log. Cosy attaches no user ID; TelemetryDeck turns a device identifier into a salted one-way hash on your iPhone or iPad so it can count distinct devices, and it can't be linked to you or your iCloud account. You can switch this off in Settings → Share anonymous usage.

Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Ideas & feedback, your message, the app version and device model (and your email, only if you type it) are sent to the developer through Apple's iCloud. Nothing from your baby's log is included.

Permissions Cosy asks for

  • Photos: to show your baby's photos, suggest firsts and save albums you choose to create. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you tap the microphone to log something by voice, when you press Talk to speak to your baby through the camera (your voice goes straight to the camera on your home Wi-Fi), and, only if you switch on wake-up alerts, while a sleep is running. Voice logging and wake-up sounds are recognised on your iPhone or iPad and never recorded or sent.
  • Camera: to scan documents, medicine labels and meals, and to take photos of firsts, when you choose to.
  • Local network: to connect to your baby camera on your home Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications and alarms: for the reminders you turn on.
  • Apple Home (optional): to show cameras you've added to the Home app.
  • Health (optional): to read a medication list from Apple Health if you choose to import it. Cosy never writes to Health.

You can change any of these in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad at any time.

Your baby camera

Cosy connects straight to your camera over your home Wi-Fi. Live video and sound go from the camera to your iPhone or iPad and nowhere else. Short clips saved around a cry or movement are kept in your family's iCloud so the grown-ups you invited can watch them, and are deleted after the number of days you choose. Cameras added from Apple Home are shown through Apple's HomeKit. Your camera maker's own app and cloud services are separate and covered by their privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app at any time. Settings → Account in Cosy deletes your account: a family member who joined leaves the family and their phone is erased; the parent who created the family can delete the whole family's data from iCloud (or hand the family to another grown-up first). Deleting Cosy removes everything stored only on your iPhone or iPad. You can also remove the family log from iCloud in Settings on your iPhone or iPad → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Cosy → Delete Data. The notification relay forgets a phone's push addresses 60 days after it last used Cosy; email us to have them removed sooner.

Children

Cosy is used by parents, about their children. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
