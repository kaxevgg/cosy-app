Cosy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Cosy is a baby diary for parents. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • We don't have your data. Cosy has no accounts on our side and no servers of its own. Your baby's log is never sent to us.
  • Your family's log lives in your own iCloud. When you invite your partner, Cosy shares the log directly between your two Apple Accounts using Apple's iCloud sharing. Only the people you invite can see it.
  • The smart features work on your iPhone. Understanding what you type or say, recognising crying, spotting wake-ups, reading food photos and finding baby photos in your library all happen on your device.
  • Gemini, only if you say yes. For long sentences or deeper answers, Cosy can ask Google's Gemini. It asks you first every time (unless you choose "Always"), and shows what will be sent. See "Gemini" below.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Ever. Cosy only sends anonymous usage counts (see below), and you can switch those off.

Gemini (optional)

Some features can use Google's Gemini through Google Firebase:

  • Reading a long sentence into log entries: only the words you typed or said, and your medicine names, are sent. Nothing from your log.
  • Deeper answers in Ask: your question, your child's age, and only the parts of the log you allowed for that question are sent, with names replaced.

Cosy asks before anything is sent, and you can choose "Never" at any time. Cosy uses Gemini on a paid plan, under which Google does not use what is sent to train its models or improve its products. Requests are checked with Apple's App Attest so only the genuine Cosy app can make them; no account, name or device identifier from Cosy is attached. Google's handling is covered by the Google Cloud and Firebase terms.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of Cosy are useful, the app sends anonymous counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. A count looks like "a feed was logged", "Memories was opened" or "Log or ask was used by voice". Each one also carries the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, language and region, time zone, and accessibility settings such as text size.

They never include names, notes, foods, amounts, times, questions, medicine names, photos or anything else from your log. Cosy attaches no user ID; TelemetryDeck turns a device identifier into a salted one-way hash on your iPhone so it can count distinct devices, and it can't be linked to you or your iCloud account. You can switch this off in Settings → Share anonymous usage.

Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Ideas & feedback, your message, the app version and iPhone model (and your email, only if you type it) are sent to the developer through Apple's iCloud. Nothing from your baby's log is included.

Permissions Cosy asks for

  • Photos: to show your baby's photos, suggest firsts and save albums you choose to create. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you tap the microphone to log something by voice, and, only if you switch on wake-up alerts, while a sleep is running. Sound is recognised on your iPhone and never recorded or sent.
  • Camera: to scan documents, medicine labels and meals, and to take photos of firsts, when you choose to.
  • Local network: to connect to your baby camera on your home Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications and alarms: for the reminders you turn on.
  • Health (optional): to read a medication list from Apple Health if you choose to import it. Cosy never writes to Health.

You can change any of these in the iPhone Settings app at any time.

Your baby camera

Cosy connects straight to your camera over your home Wi-Fi. Video and sound go from the camera to your iPhone and nowhere else. Your camera maker's own app and cloud services are separate and covered by their privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app at any time. Deleting Cosy removes everything stored only on your iPhone. To remove the family log from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Cosy → Delete Data.

Children

Cosy is used by parents, about their children. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
